Delete Account

How to permanently delete your Liftz account and data.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Liftz app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately — this cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you.

liftzadmin@gmail.com

We aim to complete these requests within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, immediately and in full — nothing is retained afterward:

  • Your profile (name, email, physical stats, preferences)
  • All workout history, routines, and readiness/insights data
  • Hunt activity, rankings, and reward history
  • Your sign-in account (Google / Apple) associated with Liftz

Any active subscription is managed separately by the App Store or Google Play — cancel it there if you don't want it to renew, since deleting your Liftz account does not automatically cancel App Store/Play Store billing.
